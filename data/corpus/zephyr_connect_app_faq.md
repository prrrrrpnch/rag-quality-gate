# Zephyr Connect App FAQ

**Which products work with the app?**
The AeroPure 500 and the RoboSweep S7 work with Zephyr Connect. The AeroPure 300 and the BrewMate K2 do not have Wi-Fi.

**Which phones are supported?**
Android 9 or later and iOS 15 or later.

**Does the app support 5 GHz Wi-Fi?**
No. All connected Zephyr Home products need a 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi network.

**How do firmware updates work?**
Firmware updates install automatically overnight between 2:00 and 4:00 when the device is idle. You can also start an update manually under Device Settings.

**Can my family control the same device?**
Yes. You can share a device with up to 5 family members from Device Settings > Share.

**Does the app work with voice assistants?**
Not yet. Voice assistant support is not available in the current version of the app.
