# AeroPure Setup and Filter Replacement Guide

This guide covers both the AeroPure 300 and the AeroPure 500.

## First-time setup
1. Remove all packaging, including the plastic bag around the filter cartridge. The purifier will not work properly with the bag left on.
2. Place the purifier on a flat, stable floor with at least 30 cm of free space on every side.
3. Plug in the unit and press the power button. The first start runs Auto mode.
4. AeroPure 500 only: open the Zephyr Connect app and add the purifier to connect it to 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi.

## When to replace the filter
- AeroPure 300: replace the ZF-300 filter about every 6 months.
- AeroPure 500: replace the ZF-500 filter about every 8 months.
- The filter indicator turns red when a replacement is due. Heavy pollution or smoking indoors can shorten filter life.

## How to replace the filter
1. Switch off and unplug the purifier.
2. Open the rear cover by pressing the two side tabs.
3. Pull out the old cartridge and remove the plastic bag from the new one.
4. Insert the new cartridge with the pull tab facing outward and close the cover.
5. Reset the filter timer: hold the filter button for 5 seconds until the indicator turns white.

Only genuine Zephyr filters are tested with the AeroPure range. Used filters cannot be washed or reused, except the AeroPure 500 pre-filter.
