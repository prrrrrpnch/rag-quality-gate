# AeroPure 300 Air Purifier — Specifications

The AeroPure 300 (model code AP300) is Zephyr Home's compact air purifier for bedrooms and small offices.

## Performance
- Recommended room size: up to 30 m².
- Clean air delivery rate (CADR): 300 m³/h.
- Noise level: 24 dB in Sleep mode and 52 dB in Turbo mode.
- Fan speeds: 3 manual speeds plus Auto, Sleep and Turbo modes.

## Filtration
- Two-stage filter: a True HEPA H13 layer and an activated carbon layer in one cartridge.
- Replacement filter part code: ZF-300.
- Filter life: about 6 months, or roughly 2,000 hours of use.

## Electrical and physical
- Rated power: 38 W.
- Input: 220–240 V AC, 50 Hz.
- Weight: 5.2 kg.
- Dimensions: 22 × 22 × 48 cm.

## Connectivity
The AeroPure 300 has no Wi-Fi and does not work with the Zephyr Connect app. It is controlled with the touch panel on top of the unit.

## Price
The launch price of the AeroPure 300 is ₹14,990.
