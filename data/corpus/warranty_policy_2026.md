# Zephyr Home Warranty Policy (2026)

This policy applies to products bought in India on or after 1 April 2026 and replaces all earlier warranty FAQs.

## Warranty periods
- AeroPure 300 and AeroPure 500 air purifiers: 2 years (24 months) from the date of purchase.
- BrewMate K2 kettle: 18 months from the date of purchase.
- RoboSweep S7 robot vacuum: 1 year on the robot and dock, and 12 months on the battery.

## Registration
Register your product in the Zephyr Connect app or on the Zephyr Home website within 30 days of purchase. Keep your invoice as proof of purchase; unregistered products are still covered if you can show the invoice.

## What is not covered
- Consumables such as air filters, brushes and mop pads are not covered by the warranty.
- Damage caused by accidents, liquid spills (except normal kettle use), power surges or unauthorised repair.
- Commercial or rental use.

## Extended warranty
An extended warranty adds 1 extra year for ₹1,499. It must be bought within 90 days of the original purchase date.

## How to claim
Check your warranty status with your product serial number, then book a service visit. Serial numbers look like ZH-AP-123456 and are printed on the label under the product.
