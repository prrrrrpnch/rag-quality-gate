# AeroPure Quick-Start Card (2024 edition)

NOTE: This card is from 2024 and has been superseded by the AeroPure Setup and Filter Replacement Guide.

- Unpack the purifier and plug it in.
- Replace the filter every 12 months on both models.
- To reset the filter timer, hold the filter button for 3 seconds.
- Keep the purifier at least 10 cm from walls.
