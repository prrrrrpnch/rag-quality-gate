# AeroPure Troubleshooting and Error Codes

## Error codes
- E1 — Fan motor fault. Unplug the purifier for 1 minute and restart it. If E1 returns, book a service visit.
- E2 — Air quality sensor is dirty (AeroPure 500 only). Clean the sensor window on the side panel with a dry cotton swab.
- E3 — Filter missing or rear cover open. Check that the filter is inserted and the rear cover clicks shut.
- E4 — Wi-Fi connection lost (AeroPure 500 only). Make sure your router broadcasts a 2.4 GHz network and re-add the purifier in the Zephyr Connect app.

## Common problems
- The purifier smells of plastic: this is normal for the first 48 hours of use. If a burning smell appears, switch off and unplug immediately and contact support.
- Airflow feels weak: check that the plastic bag was removed from the filter and that the filter is not overdue for replacement.
- The air quality ring always shows red (AeroPure 500): clean the sensor window; if it still shows red in clean air, the sensor may need service.
- The unit is louder than usual: switch from Turbo to Auto or Sleep mode. A rattling noise usually means the filter is not seated correctly.
