# RoboSweep S7 Robot Vacuum — Specifications

## Cleaning
- Suction power: up to 4000 Pa.
- Navigation: LiDAR laser navigation with multi-floor mapping (up to 3 saved maps).
- Mopping: 250 ml water tank with 3 water-flow levels.
- Dustbin capacity: 450 ml.
- Threshold climbing: up to 2 cm.
- Noise level: about 62 dB in Standard mode.

## Battery
- Battery: 5200 mAh lithium-ion.
- Runtime: up to 150 minutes in Standard mode.
- Charging time: about 4 hours.
- The robot returns to the dock automatically when the battery falls to 15% and resumes cleaning after recharging.

## Maintenance schedule
- Side brush: replace every 3–6 months.
- Main brush: replace every 6–12 months.
- HEPA filter: replace every 3 months.
- Mop pad: wash after each use and replace every 3 months.

## Connectivity
Wi-Fi 2.4 GHz only. The RoboSweep S7 is controlled with the Zephyr Connect app.
