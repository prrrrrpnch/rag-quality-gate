# RoboSweep S7 — Setup and App Pairing

## Placing the dock
Place the charging dock against a wall on a hard floor, with at least 0.5 m of clear space on each side and 1.5 m in front. Keep it out of direct sunlight.

## Pairing with the Zephyr Connect app
1. Install the Zephyr Connect app and sign in.
2. Make sure your phone is on a 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi network. 5 GHz networks are not supported.
3. Hold the Home and Power buttons together for 5 seconds until the robot says "Ready to pair".
4. Tap Add Device in the app and follow the steps.

## Maps and no-go zones
After the first full clean the robot saves a map. In the app you can split rooms, name them and draw no-go zones or virtual walls. Up to 3 floor maps can be saved.

## Resetting Wi-Fi
To reset only the Wi-Fi settings, hold the Home and Power buttons for 5 seconds again. To restore factory settings, press the reset pinhole under the top cover for 3 seconds; this erases saved maps.
