# BrewMate K2 Smart Kettle — User Manual

## Specifications
- Capacity: 1.7 litres. Minimum fill: 0.5 litres.
- Power: 2200 W.
- Body: food-grade 304 stainless steel inner body.
- Temperature presets: 40 °C, 60 °C, 70 °C, 80 °C, 90 °C and 100 °C.
- Keep-warm: holds the selected temperature for up to 60 minutes.
- Safety: automatic shut-off after boiling and boil-dry protection.
- The BrewMate K2 has no Wi-Fi and does not connect to the Zephyr Connect app.

## Using the kettle
1. Fill with water between the MIN and MAX marks.
2. Place the kettle on the power base.
3. Press the temperature button to choose a preset, then press Start.
4. Press Keep Warm before or after heating to hold the temperature.

## Descaling
Descale the BrewMate K2 every 4 weeks in hard-water areas. Fill with 1 litre of water and 1 tablespoon of citric acid, boil, leave for 30 minutes, then empty and rinse twice.

## Indicator lights
- Blue ring: heating.
- Steady white ring: keep-warm active.
- Red light blinking: the kettle was switched on empty or with too little water; boil-dry protection has cut the power. Let it cool for 10 minutes, then refill.
- No light when Start is pressed: check that the kettle is seated correctly on the base and that the base is plugged in.
