# Safety Notes for All Zephyr Home Products

## AeroPure purifiers
- Do not use the purifier in bathrooms or other wet areas.
- Keep at least 30 cm of space around the purifier and never cover the air outlet.
- Do not spray air fresheners or insecticides directly into the air intake.

## BrewMate K2 kettle
- Never immerse the kettle or its power base in water.
- Only boil water. Do not heat milk, soup or other liquids in the kettle.
- Do not open the lid while the water is boiling.
- Keep the kettle and its cord out of reach of children.

## RoboSweep S7
- Pick up cables, socks and small objects before cleaning.
- Do not use the robot outdoors or on wet floors without the mop module.
- Use only the original charging dock and battery. Do not try to open or replace the battery yourself; dispose of old batteries at an e-waste collection point.

## All products
Switch off and unplug any product that smells of burning, sparks or makes unusual noises, and contact Zephyr Home support.
