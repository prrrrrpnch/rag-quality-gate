# Returns and Refunds Policy

- You can return a product within 10 days of delivery for a full refund if it is unused and in its original packaging.
- If a product arrives damaged, report it within 48 hours of delivery with photos of the box and the product; we will send a free replacement.
- Refunds are made to the original payment method within 5–7 working days after the returned product passes inspection.
- Opened consumables such as filters and mop pads cannot be returned.
- Products bought from third-party sellers must be returned to that seller.
