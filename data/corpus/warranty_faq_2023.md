# Warranty FAQ (2023)

NOTE: This FAQ is out of date and has been superseded by the Zephyr Home Warranty Policy (2026).

**How long is the warranty on AeroPure purifiers?**
AeroPure purifiers have a 1-year warranty.

**How long is the BrewMate kettle warranty?**
The BrewMate kettle has a 1-year warranty.

**When do I need to register?**
Register within 15 days of purchase.

**Are filters covered?**
Filters are covered for the first 6 months.

**Can I buy an extended warranty?**
Extended warranties are not offered.

**What about the robot vacuum battery?**
The RoboSweep battery is covered for 6 months.

**What are support hours?**
Support is open Monday to Friday, 9:00 to 18:00.
