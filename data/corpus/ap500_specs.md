# AeroPure 500 Air Purifier — Specifications

The AeroPure 500 (model code AP500) is Zephyr Home's large-room air purifier for living rooms and open-plan spaces.

## Performance
- Recommended room size: up to 50 m².
- Clean air delivery rate (CADR): 500 m³/h.
- Noise level: 26 dB in Sleep mode and 58 dB in Turbo mode.
- Fan speeds: 4 manual speeds plus Auto, Sleep and Turbo modes.
- Air quality sensing: a laser PM2.5 sensor with a colour ring and a numeric display.

## Filtration
- Three-stage filtration: a washable pre-filter, a True HEPA H13 layer and an activated carbon layer.
- Replacement filter part code: ZF-500.
- Filter life: about 8 months, or roughly 3,000 hours of use.
- The pre-filter can be rinsed with water every 2 weeks.

## Electrical and physical
- Rated power: 55 W.
- Input: 220–240 V AC, 50 Hz.
- Weight: 7.8 kg.
- Dimensions: 30 × 30 × 62 cm.

## Connectivity and controls
- Wi-Fi: 2.4 GHz only, used by the Zephyr Connect app for remote control and schedules.
- Child lock: hold the lock button for 3 seconds.

## Price
The launch price of the AeroPure 500 is ₹22,990.
