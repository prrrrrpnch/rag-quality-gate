# RoboSweep S7 — Troubleshooting and Error Codes

## Error codes
- E10 — Drive wheel stuck. Lift the robot, remove hair or debris around the wheels and restart.
- E12 — Cliff sensor error. Wipe the four cliff sensors underneath with a dry cloth. Very dark carpets can also trigger E12.
- E15 — Main brush tangled. Remove the brush cover, cut away hair with the cleaning tool and refit the brush.
- E21 — Dustbin missing. Reinsert the dustbin until it clicks.
- E30 — Battery temperature too high or too low. Let the robot rest at room temperature (10–40 °C) for 1 hour.

## Common problems
- The robot does not charge: clean the charging contacts on the robot and the dock with a dry cloth and check that the dock is plugged in.
- The robot cannot find the dock: make sure the dock has 1.5 m of clear space in front and is not blocked by furniture.
- The app cannot find the robot: confirm the phone is on 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi and reset the robot's Wi-Fi by holding Home and Power for 5 seconds.
- Cleaning time is much shorter than 150 minutes: Max suction mode and carpets reduce runtime; if runtime is under 60 minutes in Standard mode, the battery may need service.
