# Zephyr Home Service and Repair Policy

## Booking a service visit
You can book a technician visit through the Zephyr Home support assistant, the Zephyr Connect app or the support phone line. You need the product serial number and a preferred time slot.

- Service slots run Monday to Saturday, 9:00 to 18:00, in 2-hour windows.
- You can reschedule or cancel free of charge up to 12 hours before the slot.
- Home service is available in 40 cities. Elsewhere we offer free pickup and repair.

## Charges
- Service visits for products under warranty are free, including parts covered by the warranty.
- Outside warranty, the visit fee is ₹399 plus the cost of any parts.

## Repair times
- Most home-visit repairs are finished on the same visit.
- Pickup repairs are returned within 7 working days.

## Support hours
Phone and chat support is open Monday to Saturday, 8:00 to 20:00.
